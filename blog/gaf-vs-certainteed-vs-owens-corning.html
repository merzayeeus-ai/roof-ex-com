Bay Area's Three Dominant Shingle Brands

GAF, CertainTeed, and Owens Corning are the three dominant shingle manufacturers in the Bay Area, and 90 percent of new asphalt shingle roofs installed in our region come from one of these three brands. While the products look broadly similar at the showroom level, there are meaningful differences in warranty terms, installer certification programs, wind ratings, color palette breadth, and pricing that matter when you're committing to a 25-year material decision. This comparison is based on real installation experience across hundreds of Bay Area projects—not marketing materials—and focuses on the architectural shingle category that accounts for the vast majority of residential installations in 2026.

GAF Timberline HDZ — Cost, Warranty, Best Fits

GAF Timberline HDZ is a widely used architectural shingle in the Bay Area and represents GAF's flagship architectural shingle. Material cost ranges from $130 to $170 per square (100 sq ft) at the contractor level, translating to $5,500 to $9,000 for a typical 1,500 sq ft installation including labor. Wind rating is 130 mph with proper installation (six-nail pattern, starter strip, ridge cap), making it suitable for many Bay Area locations including Pacifica and ridge-top neighborhoods. Warranty terms depend on the selected product, installation, registration, and current GAF eligibility requirements; request the specific GAF warranty document rather than relying on a generic year count. Color selection includes 14 colors specific to the Bay Area market with strong availability of weathered wood, charcoal, slate, and pewter gray—the most popular Bay Area colors.

CertainTeed Landmark Pro — Cost, Warranty, Best Fits

CertainTeed Landmark Pro and Landmark Premium represent CertainTeed's mid-to-premium architectural shingle line and are particularly strong in Peninsula and South Bay markets. Material cost runs $135 to $180 per square ($5,800 to $9,500 typical install). Wind rating reaches 130 mph for Landmark Pro and 130 mph for Landmark Premium with proper installation. CertainTeed warranty terms vary by product, installer credential, registration, and current program rules, so request the applicable written document and confirm whether labor coverage is included. CertainTeed's color palette is the broadest of the three brands with over 20 color options, particularly strong in earth-tone variants (Burnt Sienna, Heather Blend, Cobblestone Gray) that suit Mediterranean and Spanish-style Bay Area architecture. The Landmark Premium line offers a thicker, more dimensional look that approaches designer shingle aesthetics at a mid-tier price.

Owens Corning Duration — Cost, Warranty, Best Fits

Owens Corning Duration is the third major option in the Bay Area market and offers some genuinely differentiated technology. Material cost is $125 to $165 per square ($5,300 to $8,800 typical install), positioning it as the most price-competitive of the three premium brands. Wind rating is 130 mph standard, but Owens Corning's SureNail Technology (a fabric strip woven into the nail zone) provides what's marketed as 'mechanical' fastening that has demonstrated superior wind uplift resistance in independent testing. Owens Corning warranty terms vary by product, contractor credential, registration, and current program rules; confirm the applicable document and whether any labor coverage applies before choosing a system. Color selection is more limited (12 to 14 colors typical) but covers the most-requested Bay Area colors.

Real-World Performance Differences

Real-world performance differences between the three brands in Bay Area conditions are subtler than marketing suggests but do exist. All three brands meet Class A fire ratings and Title 24 cool roof requirements with standard product lines. In our installation experience: GAF Timberline HDZ shows the most consistent color uniformity from bundle to bundle (important for large roofs); CertainTeed Landmark Pro has the slightly thicker shadow line that homeowners notice as 'more dimensional'; Owens Corning Duration's SureNail strip does demonstrate measurably better wind uplift resistance during installation testing. Algae resistance is comparable across all three brands' algae-resistant lines (GAF Timberline HDZ AR, CertainTeed StreakFighter, Owens Corning Duration AR)—all use copper or zinc granules to inhibit Gloeocapsa magma growth that's particularly problematic in Bay Area fog belt neighborhoods. Hail performance (rare but possible in Bay Area winter storms) favors Owens Corning Duration Storm and CertainTeed Landmark IR, both Class 4 impact-rated.

How to Choose: Brand vs Installer Certification

Choosing between the three brands often comes down to the product, installation quality, and the contractor's current certification status. Manufacturer programs have different eligibility and registration requirements, and no generic year count guarantees coverage for every project. Selected systems may include up to 50 years of manufacturer/material coverage, separate from ROOF EXPRESS's 25-year workmanship warranty for qualifying new installations. When evaluating estimates, ask which manufacturer certifications each contractor currently holds, request the specific warranty document the manufacturer would issue (not just the contractor's marketing material), and verify certification status on the manufacturer's website (gaf.com, certainteed.com, owenscorning.com). All three brands offer products suited to Bay Area conditions—the bigger differentiator is matching the product to the home and confirming the complete written warranty and workmanship terms.

Key takeaways

  • Selected systems may include up to 50 years of manufacturer/material coverage; compare the product warranty with ROOF EXPRESS's separate 25-year workmanship warranty
