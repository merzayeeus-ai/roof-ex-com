Maintenance Overview

Regular roof maintenance is the most cost-effective way to extend your roof's lifespan and prevent expensive emergency repairs. This checklist is tailored to Bay Area climate conditions and seasonal patterns.

Spring Checklist

Spring (March-April): Inspect for winter storm damage (missing shingles, lifted flashing, gutter damage). Clean gutters of winter debris. Check attic for moisture, mold, or pest intrusion. Clear debris from roof valleys and around penetrations. Verify all pipe boots and vent caps are intact.

Summer Checklist

Summer (June-August): Trim tree branches hanging over or near the roof (maintain 10-foot clearance). Check for sun damage on south and west-facing slopes. Inspect attic ventilation (feel for airflow at ridge and soffit vents). Look for wasp or bird nests near eaves and vents. Note any areas where shingles are curling or showing wear.

Fall Checklist

Fall (October-November): Clean gutters and downspouts before rainy season (critical timing). Verify downspouts discharge away from foundation. Check flashing around chimneys, skylights, and walls. Clear debris from flat roof drains if applicable. Schedule professional inspection if roof is over 15 years old.

Winter Checklist

Winter (December-February): After major storms, do a ground-level visual check for damage. Check attic for active leaks after heavy rain. Verify gutters are flowing freely (not overflowing). Monitor for ice dam formation in microclimate-affected areas. Keep records of any issues noticed for spring professional inspection.

Annual Professional Inspection

Annual professional inspection: We recommend a professional inspection every 1-2 years (annually for roofs over 15 years old). Professional inspectors catch issues that aren't visible from the ground and can assess remaining roof life. Our annual inspection program includes a written report, photo documentation, and priority scheduling for any recommended repairs.

Key takeaways

  • Seasonal maintenance is the most cost-effective way to extend roof lifespan
  • Fall gutter cleaning before rainy season is the single most critical task
  • Maintain 10-foot tree clearance and check attic after every major storm
  • Professional inspection every 1–2 years; annually for roofs over 15 years old
