Complete Removal of the Existing Roof A quality roof replacement starts with proper preparation.

For a complete reroofing project, the existing roofing materials are removed so the roof deck underneath can be inspected.

This process may include removal and disposal of:

Existing shingles

Old roofing underlayment

Loose or deteriorated flashing

Roofing nails and fasteners

Damaged roofing accessories

Construction debris

Removing the old roof gives our roofing team the opportunity to inspect the condition underneath instead of simply covering existing problems. Roof Deck Inspection After tear-off, the exposed roof deck is carefully inspected.

We look for issues such as:

Rotten plywood

Water-damaged sheathing

Soft roof decking

Dry rot

Delamination

Previous improper repairs

Structural damage

A new shingle roof should always be installed over a solid and properly prepared roofing surface.

If damaged plywood is discovered, the deteriorated areas should be replaced before the new roofing system is installed. New Roofing Underlayment After the roof deck is prepared, new roofing underlayment is installed.

Underlayment provides an additional protective barrier between the roof deck and the finished shingles.

A complete roofing system should not rely only on the shingles visible from the street.

The roofing assembly works together as a system:

Roof Deck → Underlayment → Flashing → Shingles → Ventilation → Drainage

When Should You Replace Your Shingle Roof? San Jose homeowners should consider having their roof inspected when they notice:

Missing shingles

Cracked shingles

Curling shingles

Significant granule loss

Repeated leaks

Interior ceiling stains

Damaged flashing

Soft roof decking

Multiple previous repairs

An aging roofing system

Not every roofing issue requires complete replacement.

A localized problem may sometimes be repaired.

However, when the roof is deteriorating throughout, a complete replacement may provide a more reliable long-term solution. Roof Repair vs. Shingle Roof Replacement A roof repair may be appropriate when:

Damage is limited to a small area

The roof is relatively new

Most shingles remain in good condition

One flashing or penetration is causing the problem

A complete roof replacement may make more sense when:

The roof has multiple leaks

Shingles are deteriorated throughout

Repairs have been performed repeatedly

The roofing system is approaching the end of its expected service life

Significant roof-deck damage is present

Contact Roof Express today for your San Jose shingle roof replacement estimate. Frequently Asked QuestionsWhat type of shingles are best for San Jose homes? Architectural asphalt shingles are a popular choice because they provide attractive appearance, durability, multiple color options, and strong manufacturer warranty options.

How long does a shingle roof replacement take? Many residential shingle roof projects can be completed within several working days. The exact timeline depends on roof size, pitch, complexity, weather, decking repairs, skylights, and inspection requirements.

Should the old shingles be removed? A complete tear-off allows the roof deck to be inspected before the new roofing system is installed. Whether tear-off is required depends on existing conditions, applicable requirements, and the proposed roofing system.

What happens if rotten plywood is found? Deteriorated roof decking should be addressed before the new roofing system is installed. Your roofing proposal should explain how additional decking repairs are handled.

Can skylights be replaced during reroofing? Yes. Roof replacement is often an excellent time to replace aging skylights because the new skylight flashing can be properly integrated with the new roofing system.

Do new shingles come with a warranty? Most major roofing manufacturers offer material warranties, while the roofing contractor may also provide a separate workmanship warranty. Homeowners should review both before choosing a roofing system.
