A Complete Roofing System — Not Just New Shingles A quality roof replacement involves much more than simply installing shingles.

At Roof Express, we focus on the complete roofing system. Every roof is different, which is why we evaluate each property individually before recommending the appropriate roofing system. Protecting Vulnerable Areas of the Roof Waterproofing is particularly important around areas where water is more likely to enter the roofing system.

These areas can include roof valleys, eaves, plumbing penetrations, vents, skylights, chimneys, roof-to-wall transitions, and other penetrations.

Proper flashing and waterproofing help reduce the possibility of future leaks and moisture-related damage. Roof Ventilation Matters A roofing system also needs appropriate ventilation.
